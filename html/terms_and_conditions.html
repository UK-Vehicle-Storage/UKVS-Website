8. Insurance

We hold a fully comprehensive motor trade insurance policy to cover all of our responsibilities in respect of customer's vehicles whilsty in our storage care which also include road risks (customers' vehicles driven by us). Our vehicle transporters are fully comprehensively insured, which includes goods in transit insurance (customer's vehicles transported by us). This includes C M R Insurance cover whihc is a legal requirement for the carriage of goods (customers' vehicles transported) on international European journeys by road.

Owners should notify their insurance company of their intentions to store/place their vehicle(s) and/or any belongings and goods in our care. We can provide insurance cover through our own insurers to extend our cover in respect of high values or certain conditions. If an additional premium is levied upon the company for this special extension then that premium will be passed on at cost to the customer. Alternatively clients can choose to have their vehicles insured on an individual agreed policy at a more favourable rate.

Please note:

We must be made aware of vehicles that have a unique value.

We must be made aware of vehicles that have, or require, any special conditions of insurance cover.

WARNING. Do not expect you conventional comprehensive insurance, private or business (including motor trade policies), to include cover outside of your normal day to day private or business use and activities. You should notify your insurance company of you intentions to store, or to have you vehicle transported, (or both) if you intend to use your existing insurance policy to cover any of the benefits that might not be covered by our insurance. This advice applied to any company that you employ to store and/or transport your vehicle that may not have the appropriate insurance cover that you may require for you vehicle.
