Additional Services

We can offer the full spectrum of services for your vehicle. This includes:

Mechanical Service (at a dealer of your choice)

MOT's

Bodywork

Alloy Wheel Refurbishment

Tuning and Performance Upgrades

However if the maintenance or modification you require isn’t specifically mentioned please ask as we have a lot of experience, along with contacts who are used to unusual requests. We have previously accommodated power steering into a 1928 Bentley, air-conditioning into a Morris 1000 traveller, uprated brakes in a 1932 Alvis. At the other end of the scale we have done an engine rebuild on a Ferrari 512BB uplifting the power by almost 300 BHP. We have also arranged a 150 BHP upgrade on a 2014 F10 BMW M5. The jobs can be much simpler such as converting the seats in a 2001 Nissan Micra K11 to type R seats from a Honda EP3 including fabrication of the required seatbelt anchorages.
